About Us

Since 2011

Established in 2011, OSCEC is a trusted provider of professional hygiene and cleaning solutions, serving businesses throughout Malaysia and expanding into international markets. Backed by over a decade of industry expertise and extensive experience in the food and beverage sector, we are committed to helping our customers achieve the highest standards of hygiene, safety, and operational excellence.

At OSCEC, continuous improvement is at the core of everything we do. By combining industry knowledge, innovative solutions, and premium-quality products, we deliver reliable cleaning solutions tailored to the evolving needs of businesses. Our mission is to build long-term partnerships by providing exceptional value, consistent quality, and outstanding customer service.
